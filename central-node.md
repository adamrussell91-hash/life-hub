## 📏 Writing Rules (All Agents Must Follow)
*Read and obey these rules before updating any section of this page.*
1. **No duplication.** If information exists in another section of this page, do not restate it.
2. **Today's Status** is a dashboard. Totals and flags only. No timestamps, no narrative, no meal breakdowns, no motivational commentary. Max 150 words.
3. **Cross-Agent Coordination** uses one-line directives only. No essays, no restating medical context from Constraints. Purge once actioned.
4. **Recent Agent Actions** keeps a 48-hour rolling window. One line per action. Purge entries older than 48 hours.
5. **This Week** covers key events and weekly averages only. No day-by-day exercise logs. No duplicating Long-Term Trends.
6. **This Month** covers upcoming events and active goals only. No restating diagnoses or blood results from Constraints.
7. **Long-Term Trends** is for behavioural patterns and trajectories only. Medical facts live in Constraints.
8. **Constraints & Priorities** is the single source of truth for medical protocols, dietary rules and medication. All other sections reference it, never duplicate it.
9. **General tone:** Concise. No exclamation marks. No hype. No motivational language. State facts. Be brief.
10. **Today's Status staleness:** Today's Status must always include a date stamp in its heading (e.g. "Today's Status (Monday 23 March 2026)"). Any Today's Status dated more than 24 hours ago is stale. Clare's Morning Sweep will rewrite it from first principles when stale. All other agents: if Today's Status appears stale, proceed from the Constraints section data rather than the potentially outdated Status flags.
