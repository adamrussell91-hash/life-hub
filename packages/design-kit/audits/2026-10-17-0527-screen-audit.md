## Eyebrows

Kit eyebrow is 13px, weight 700, uppercase, 0.08em tracking, `--muted`.

Knowledge protocols override `.page-header__eyebrow` to `#e4c98a` and `letter-spacing: .14em` (`protocols/style.css`). The confirm card drops uppercase and uses 12px at weight 500. Life groups `.eyebrow`, `.section-kicker`, and `.page-header__eyebrow` onto one rule, so in-page kickers match the page eyebrow.

## Colour

`tokens.css` is about thirty named colours. The stylesheets contain 498 distinct hex, rgb, and hsl literals. `color.md` lists each literal and every declaration.

The kit is part of that count. Calendar, confirm card, and chat styles in `packages/design-kit` use `#fff`, 10.5px label fills, and raw shadows. Hub chrome on top of that includes Life status greens and reds and unread `#E5484D`, Knowledge protocol gold `#e4c98a` on near-black, shelf sea colours, Tasks grove sky `#a6dbf2`, and Travel's repeated navy and high-sea literals next to `--font-fun`.
