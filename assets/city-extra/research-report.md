## Candidates not committed

- Quaternius QAL packs: not committed where offered under QAL or without a page-level CC0 grant, because the public repo redistributes raw source files.
- Poly Pizza Cruise Ship (https://poly.pizza/m/yq9EKmEmfC): CC0 but a cruise ship, not the requested small harbour ferry.
- Poly Pizza transmission-tower crane search results: CC0 but a tower rather than construction crane; does not meet the brief.

## Asset Forge findings

Official Asset Forge page checked 2026-10-08: Standard is **US$19.95** and Deluxe is **US$39.95** (one-time, DRM-free). Its block system can plausibly build the bus, school bus, tower crane, and food truck in a consistent Kenney-like style. It exports glTF among its common 3D formats; the page does not explicitly promise binary GLB. No purchase was made.
