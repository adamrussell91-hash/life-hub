The dial grows complications

Your green dial stays exactly as it is. These faces add what a watch would: a dive bezel for medication, a GMT hand for Seoul and Corey, a moon for the term, and the day's weather in the quiet band. Swipe or use the arrows to change face; tap the centre to flip to the caseback.
