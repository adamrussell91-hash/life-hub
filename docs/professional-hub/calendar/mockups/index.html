  • Promise record: who keeps theirs, across the whole case.
  • One button exports a case summary for a Learning Support or parent meeting.

Events · one page, and PD has a shape

Every event is a block page with the round "+", and photos are just a gallery block. Turn on Counts as PD and you choose its shape: one-off (a single Zoom or day), series (sessions weeks apart) or program (a multi-day seminar with many talks). Every talk or activity can become its own Knowledge Hub note, and its hours roll up to the session and then the whole series.

What's gone
  • The "Retry incomplete Task link" button. The learning task links in the background, and you only see a small amber dot if it's still failing after an hour.
  • The large photo panel. Photos go in a gallery block if you want them.
  • The PD dashboard stays as it is. It reads hours from these pages.
