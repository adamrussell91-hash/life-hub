# Grove wildlife across visible weekly zones

User approved the described Year/Term wildlife upgrade with “do it”. Build on the existing TypeScript/Three.js annual renderer. No extra downloads or backend record writes.

## Behaviour

Year and Term show earned wildlife moving between suitable completed weekly regions in the displayed period. An open region contains at least one live dated completion within the displayed period through min(period end, today). Empty and future regions are not opened. Earned milestones are evaluated through that same cutoff, rather than the Year view anchor; an animal may use only weeks whose end is on/after its earned date. Disconnected open habitats stay disconnected; do not cut through locked weeks, water, steep land or trunks to force a connection. Day/Week retain close local encounters.

Land routes use safe geographic connections and prefer species habitat: deer/moose clearings, squirrels/chipmunks trees, fox/wolf wider roaming. Birds fly between legal dry landing spots, lifting above obstacles during flights. Mallards/otters use connected actual lake/river surfaces, respect the footprint and elevation, and never cross dry gaps or waterfalls by interpolating a straight shortcut. Roaming is actual destination selection with travel, feed/rest pauses and subsequent destinations, not a small fixed out-and-back loop. Babies trail their parent along the same safe route without crossing corners.

The actor population is shared across the view and capped at 11, never multiplied by 53 weeks. Two extra companions rotate by date. All 20 delivered animal IDs have a reachable encounter rule: original stag accompanies the deer milestone; purchased deer/stag can alternate the deer model, purchased fox the fox model, purchased rabbit the hare model. Preserve original milestone species and earn dates, baby rules, publisher clip meanings, catalogue failure handling and credits data without restoring the removed credits control. Alternate models must be deterministically reachable, not dead constants; birds/swimmers and habitat derive from species even when the model ID changes.

Panning/zooming does not recreate wildlife or reset its state. Pause, reduced motion, offscreen/hidden handling and zero-delta renders freeze route and animation clocks. Offscreen actors avoid expensive mixer work. All navigation and destination planning happens outside the per-frame loop; navigation preparation must yield or run off-thread so dense histories do not block interaction. Route resources/loading are abortable when the view changes/disposes. No live graph or actor cache may retain disposed scenes. Keep stable terrain/tree allocation unchanged.

## Validation

Real geography fixtures and controlled small geographic fixtures must prove multi-region travel, disconnected/locked/future exclusion, safe segments and tree clearance, valid connected swimming, safe bird landings, per-species habitat preference, earned-date checks and all-model reachability. Motion tests prove destination changes, pause/zero delta, corner-safe baby trailing and culling clock consistency. Integration tests prove Year/Term use period cutoff and multiple regions; Day/Week stay local; population<=11; cancellation/failed models dispose safely. Native Chrome and WebKit checks at 1440 and 390 must show wide wildlife routes across completed regions with no errors or regressions, and exercise pan/zoom/pause/disposal. Dense-history performance should stay within the existing desktop target; report actual measurements, never call emulation physical phone acceptance. Run mandatory pre-PR gate and production build, independent reviews, then publish and verify live.
