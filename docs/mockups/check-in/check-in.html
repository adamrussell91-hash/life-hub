Check-in guide · mockup

A second guide on the Meeting Guides shelf, for when a wellbeing conversation comes up with a student. Invented student; every line of step wording here is PLACEHOLDER — the real text loads at runtime from the private data repo and is never stored on a record. Nothing on screen says “crisis”; there is no red.
