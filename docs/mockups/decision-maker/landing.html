    How a session builds

    Frame, then the coach discerns the models that fit. The book is a catalogue, so the coach chooses.

    1 · FRAME

    Three questions

    What you are deciding, who it is about and what you want, and what you already know.

    2 · EXPLORE

    The coach picks a model

    An Eisenhower matrix for competing demands, a rubber band when you are pulled two ways.

    3 · DECIDE

    Go forward

    Name the decision and what would change your mind, so it can be checked later.

    4 · REVIEW

    Look back

    A record on its task or project page, and a review task to compare what you expected with what happened.

    The four sections

    The book's four parts, as a compass: about you or others, to understand or to improve. A few models from each.
