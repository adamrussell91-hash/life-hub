Fleet week

Fleet week · a week-long retrieval campaign for two anonymous teams of two. Every square on the 5 × 5 chart is a question about one character and one theme from an invented novella, The Lighthouse Keeper’s Daughter. Pick a screen above. Calm shows the full reduced-motion version (your system setting does the same). All students, names and answers are invented.

Spec §1.2 · §1.3 placement

Hide your two ships

Your partner’s two destroyers are already on your team’s chart. You place the frigate (3) and the cutter (1).

  • Pick a ship, then tap the square for its bow. Rotate switches across and down.
  • Ships can’t overlap or touch your partner’s ships. A bad square says why.
  • On a laptop, hover to preview.
  • Unplaced ships are placed by the app at the cut-off.
Student phone · Thursday, round 3
Spec §1.3 · §1.4 · §1.9

Four shells, one at a time

  • Tap a square in the rival’s fog. Then choose Sure or Unsure.
  • Only then does the question appear, with a 60-second ring. One typed try. Paste is off.
  • The shell flies, but nothing lands until the round closes. The square shows a pending ring.
  • D1 is a returning square: your dud from Tuesday, back today with the other question.
  • The teal band is a sonar ping: a rival was sure and wrong, so you know a ship is in row E.
  • Hit or missed squares can’t be fired at again. Duds can, by either of you.
Student phone · Wednesday morning
Spec §1.4 · §1.8 · §2

The dawn report

Tuesday’s round closed at 8:00 pm and resolved overnight. Every line uses this viewer’s call signs: here you are Commander Lark and your partner is Captain Pike. On your partner’s phone the same people have different names.

The sun rises and warms the report. In Calm it is simply morning.

Student phone · Wednesday
Spec §1.6

Confirm your partner’s hit

The partner sees the exact question their partner was asked and the answer they typed. They write one line on why it is right. There is no model answer.

  • Filed within one round: the hit counts in full.
  • Late: the hit sticks at half damage.
  • The teacher can reject a weak line once; it comes back here.
Student phone · Thursday, first visit
Spec §1.5

The sinking blow

You landed the last hit on a ship, so at your next visit you get its recall line: type the quote from memory, under the writing watch.

Right: the ship sinks with a bonus. Wrong: it still sinks at the next report, with no bonus, so matches never stall. The result arrives with the dawn report, like every other shot.
