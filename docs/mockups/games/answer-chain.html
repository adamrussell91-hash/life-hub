Answer Chain

One write. One shadow. The rest is stealing.

You're the teal triangle. Four groups, four chains, all starting from photosynthesis and running at once. Each round, every chain gets one new link. Across the game you get one WRITE, one SHADOW, and every other round is a STEAL. The strip at the top of the phone shows your whole game, with the current round lit up.

  1. WRITE · it's your turn in your chain. Write the question (20 s) or the one-word answer (10 s).
  2. SHADOW · a teammate is answering. Answer the same question secretly. Match them and you both look clever; wager 1–3×.
  3. STEAL · any other time. You get another group's newest question and the letter count of its answer. Guess the word and you CAPTURE it.

Shadow is always your own chain. Steal is always someone else's.

REVIEW · after the reveal, everyone sees 10 anonymous question–answer pairs. Fix up to 3 (type the better answer) or flag a question as unclear, and star the most accurate one. Only fixes that 3+ classmates agree on, and the teacher confirms, go back to the writer.
