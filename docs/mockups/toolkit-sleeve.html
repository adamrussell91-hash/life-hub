Thinking Toolkit as a sleeve

A toolkit card no longer sits on the page as a separate block. It slides over the question students are already answering: a thin coloured edge, a tint on their answer box, and three short steps. The question is the task. The sleeve shows how to do it well.

The question leadsThe sleeve sits on top of a question or activity. It never sets its own task.
One line when closedChip, goal, progress. Open it only when you need it.
See it · Do it · Check itExplicit instruction: a model, one move, then a self-check against their own writing.
Support / ExtendSame three steps, two levels. The student switches; the teacher sets the starting level.
