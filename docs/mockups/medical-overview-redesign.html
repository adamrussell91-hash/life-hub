Medical Overview — 3 redesigns

Static mockups using your real entries (lab numbers partly illustrative). Each one fixes the four complaints: everything looks equally important, no future, no mini lab view, Sara's notes disappearing. Scroll for A → B → C, then the recommendation at the bottom.

Why your 26 Sep cold update is invisible (found in code, not a design issue): resolveMedicalLogCandidate in medical-normalize.js fuzzy-matches Sara's new note to the 24/09 "Sore throat, sniffles, poor sleep" record and, because it's ≤3 days away (APPEND_DATE_SLOP_DAYS), merges it into that record and keeps the old date. So it's in the notes of the 24/09 card, not a new dot. And the page can show future events (medical-model.js splits future/past) — but Sara writes MRCP, colonoscopy, March review, Stelara dose etc. as prose inside a note instead of as dated records, and follow_up_date is only shown in the side sheet. All 3 designs need the same data fix: episodes (a note appends a dated entry to an episode rather than overwriting) + planned items as real future records + a weight field (major / routine / minor).

Concept B

Condition Lanes — a horizontal health "subway map"

One lane per condition thread (IBD, Liver, Mind, Acute). You see the story of each, not a pile of cards.
Biomarker ribbons sit inside their lane: calprotectin falls, GGT stays high, against a shaded normal band.
Today is a vertical line; the right side is the future — planned doses and procedures as hollow markers.
Pinch/drag to zoom weeks ↔ years. Colds are thin grey bars — visible, but quiet.

Tap any marker → the same detail sheet you have today, with the mini lab panel from A. Solid = happened, hollow = planned, square = specialist visit.
