Postcards on check-ins

You tick a stop safe, add a photo and up to 30 seconds of voice. The people on your travel link get a postcard that turns itself over.

The postcard

The photo shows for a moment, then the card turns over. Tap the card to turn it back.

Sample postcard

Sample card with a drawn stand-in for the photo. There is no sound here: the play button runs 12 seconds in place of a 28-second clip.

Address
Where the photo was taken, read from the photo's own location. Street and neighbourhood, never a house number. You can change it before posting.
Stamp
The city scene for the leg you are on, with the day of the trip.
Postmark
The leg's ink colour, the city, and the local date and time of the check-in.
Words
Your voice, transcribed. You read and fix them before anyone else sees them.
Voice
Up to 30 seconds, played from the button on the card.

Where followers find it

The newest postcard sits at the top of the public link with the others stacked behind it. The stop you ticked also carries its postcard, where today's photo thumbnail sits.
