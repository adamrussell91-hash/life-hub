# Cognitive Protocols — Graphic Requirements and AI Art Brief

## Shared visual contract

**Reference treatment.** Match the supplied reference image: an editorial illustrated portrait, waist-up, clean black/brown ink contour, soft flat colour with a few textured brush accents, off-white ground, elegant but contemporary. Faces are expressive, proportionate and human; avoid photorealism, chibi, 3D render, glossy game art, visual clutter, text baked into artwork, logos and watermarks.

**Composition.** Generate each voice as a **waist-up, vertical 2:3 portrait**, subject centred with a little headroom, hands optional and kept below the chest where possible. Deliver transparent PNG/WebP if supported; otherwise use a quiet warm-ivory ground. Leave 12% clear margin around the figure and keep the lower third visually calm for card labels. Generate characters separately, never as a group scene.

**Card system.** Card fronts are 2:3 portrait cards with a double ornamental rule, numbered corner, central illustration and a small origin label. Card backs share a navy, ivory and muted-gold deck pattern, but each carries a barely visible protocol-specific motif. Backgrounds are full-page 16:9 atmospheric illustrations behind the card deck: low contrast, wide negative space at centre, and no readable text. The established hub type, navigation and buttons remain unchanged.

**Prompt suffix for every image.** `editorial illustrated portrait for a sophisticated knowledge app, waist-up, clean expressive ink linework, limited warm ivory navy muted gold and soft pastel palette, subtle paper texture, refined contemporary anime-inspired character design, white-to-ivory background, no text, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no full body, no cropped face`

Appearance specifications below are **visual interpretations of source-defined roles and temperament** unless the Notion source explicitly supplies staging. They should be treated as art direction, not historic or biographical fact.

## 1. The Three Fates

**Source anchor:** Greek thread imagery; Clotho is breathless, associative and generative; Atropos is dry, unhurried and evidence-led; Lachesis is still, precise and architectural; The Weave is solemn, unhurried and witnesses tensions.

- **Background generation:** `an abstract Greek textile field, four fine red-gold threads crossing an ivory paper ground, a faint circular loom and subtle classical geometric border at the extreme edges, restrained navy shadow, large quiet centre, no figures, no text`.
- **Card front:** ivory card with a muted-gold double rule; central spindle and four threads forming a measured knot. Corner number `01`; origin label `Greek threads` added in HTML, never generated into the image.
- **Card back:** deep navy woven-thread pattern with four tiny gold thread ends meeting at a central star; soft paper grain, no lettering.
- **Clotho:** `warm young Greek-inspired spinner, waist-up, animated hands holding a spindle and bright red thread, loose dark curls, alert eyes and forward posture, cream chiton with a coral woven trim, lively gestural ink marks`.
- **Atropos:** `composed Greek-inspired cutter, waist-up, dark hair pinned cleanly back, unsmiling direct gaze, fine shears held precisely beside a taut thread, charcoal peplos with restrained bronze clasp, sparse surgical linework`.
- **Lachesis:** `still Greek-inspired measurer, waist-up, mature woman with a measuring cord and small wax tablet, balanced symmetrical posture, dark indigo drapery with a measured gold edge, calm architectural expression`.
- **The Weave:** `solemn and androgynous Greek-inspired witness, waist-up, hands loosely gathering four threads without pulling them together, pale layered drapery, quiet lowered gaze, fine halo-like loom circle behind the head`.

## 2. The Horizon Council

**Source anchor:** a long hall, fire, three figures already seated. Ketill is gruff with weathered hands and tends the fire; Alvar sits furthest out in shadow; Sigrid listens with eyes closed, and the temperature drops when she opens them.

- **Background generation:** `wide Norse long hall at dusk, low hearth fire at left, timber beams receding into blue-grey shadow, three empty high-backed seats suggested in silhouette, snow light through a narrow opening, quiet central negative space, restrained, not fantasy game art`.

- **Card back:** navy herringbone textile with a faint longhouse roofline and one warm ember at centre.
- **Ketill the Hearthkeeper:** `weathered Norse hearthkeeper, waist-up, rough wool and practical leather apron, weathered hands tending a small iron fire tool, sturdy seated lean forward, grey-brown beard, firelight on one cheek, direct unsentimental gaze`.

## 3. The Refinery

- **Background generation:** `quiet workshop of argument and evidence, abstract brass armature, drafting grid and three incomplete geometric forms on warm ivory paper, a small forge glow, wide clean centre, academic rather than industrial`.
- **Card front:** sage-and-ivory card with three stacked diamonds: assembled, split, then rejoined with a visible seam. Number `03`; HTML label `Chevruta paired argument`.
- **Card back:** navy drafting-paper pattern, thin gold construction lines and a central hexagonal seal.
- **The Builder:** `scholarly craftsperson, waist-up, rolled sleeves and neat vest, carefully placing labelled but unreadable geometric blocks on a drafting board, attentive confident posture, warm umber and sage accents, constructive energy`.

## 5. The Mirror Council

## 7. The Witness

**Source anchor:** Process Trace reconstructs without claiming ground truth; Pattern Match distinguishes warranted pattern from noise; Recalibration assesses confidence and uncertainty. The protocol pauses if its findings land as distress.

- **Background generation:** `minimal contemplative observation room, concentric rings and a single clear glass bowl on ivory paper, soft window light, traces of a pencil timeline, pale sage and navy, quiet and non-therapeutic, large negative space`.
- **Card front:** sage-and-ivory card with three concentric observation rings, four cardinal ticks and a central point. Number `07`; HTML label `Zen and Vipassana observation`.
- **Card back:** navy field with faint concentric circles, sparse gold points and a single centred eye-like aperture; no mystical symbols.
- **Process Trace:** `calm process observer, waist-up, neutral contemporary linen shirt, holding a simple sequential timeline strip, attentive nonjudgmental face, no clinical costume, soft sage background`.
- **Pattern Match:** `careful analyst, waist-up, dark navy overshirt, looking through a simple clear lens at two partially overlapping dot patterns, measured sceptical expression, restrained linework`.
- **Recalibration:** `quiet calibrator, waist-up, cream and charcoal clothing, holding a small balance scale with one side deliberately unweighted, open honest expression, pale gold accent, no certainty theatrics`.

## 8. The Tribunal of Frames

**Source anchor:** three independent reframes: hidden function, scale shift, and context shift. They never debate or synthesize; they present parallel possibilities.

- **Background generation:** `abstract room made of nested architectural frames, one small doorway and one large doorway showing the same distant horizon, overlapping translucent planes on ivory paper, navy shadow and muted blue, no figures, no text`.
- **Card front:** blue-and-ivory card with three offset rectangular frames and one central horizon line. Number `08`; HTML label `Nested frames and scale`.
- **Card back:** navy grid of offset frames, a small gold square held inside a larger square, restrained paper grain.
- **The Inverter:** `incisive systems thinker, waist-up, dark high-collared jacket, holding an object that reads differently when inverted, composed probing gaze, clean angular ink lines, no sinister styling`.
- **The Scaler:** `practical visual analyst, waist-up, soft blue work jacket, one hand framing a tiny model and the other indicating a large map beyond it, alert curious expression, balanced geometric composition`.
- **The Context Shifter:** `calm contextual thinker, waist-up, layered ivory and navy clothing, standing before two overlapping doorframe shapes, gaze turned toward an alternate opening, poised and non-prescriptive`.

## Delivery checklist

- Generate one 16:9 background, one card-front illustration, one card-back texture, and one portrait per named voice.
- Keep text, card titles, labels, borders and controls in the web UI; do not ask an image model to render them.
- Maintain each protocol's visual motif across all assets, while preserving legibility at 320 px card width.
- Run an accessibility review: front/back state is announced by the button, illustration is decorative when title and origin already convey the same meaning, and reduced-motion disables the deal and flip animation.
