Placements hidden

Try it: watch symbols arrive (they roam the whole space, nothing to do with where they placed). Reveal, click any symbol, show all reasons, sort them, then let them re-place. Statement 3 is a snapped binary to force a defence.

Student state

Student side

  • Tap the line to drop your symbol, drag it, or nudge it with the arrows. On a snapped line it jumps to the nearest stop.
  • A reason is required before you can place it.
  • Students never see anyone else's position before the reveal. The waiting screen shows only their own.
  • Student paced: after placing, “Next statement” moves them on. Teacher reveals each statement when ready.
  • Re-place: their first spot stays as a dashed ghost. Moving needs a “why I moved” reason. “Keep it here” is one tap.
