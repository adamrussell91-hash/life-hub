# Cursor UI failure register

**What this is.** A running list of every UI/UX mistake Cursor has shipped in this repo more than once, or shipped while claiming it was done. Each entry has the rule that prevents it and a **check** that proves it's fixed. Nothing counts as fixed on "looks right to me".

**Who uses it**
- **Cursor:** `.cursor/rules/ui-failure-register.mdc` makes you read this before any UI work. Before you tick any UI item, run the check for every entry that applies to your change.
- **Claude, when writing a build brief:** copy the IDs relevant to the feature into the brief's "failure modes" section (e.g. `L1 L2 S1 V1 D2 P1`), with a feature-specific check for each.
- **Claude, when reviewing Cursor's work:** every new failure gets an entry here in the same PR as the review. A repeat failure gets its **Seen** line extended; repeats are the point of the list.

**Entry format:** `ID · name`: **Seen:** where it happened. **Rule:** what to do. **Check:** how to prove it.

---

## L: Layout and width

**L1 · Column doesn't fill the page.** **Seen:** Goals cards #495; Goals v2 #488; Medical river v2 (~410px column with 60% of the page empty). **Rule:** Grid tracks are `minmax(0, Nfr)`, never bare `1fr`. Text-holding flex/grid children get `min-width: 0`. Don't put a fixed `width`/`max-width` on a main column unless the brief asks for one; to cap the reading width, cap the text inside the card, not the column. **Check:** in DevTools at 1440, sibling blocks on the page (card row, strip, list) share the same right edge ±1px.

**L2 · Cards with dead space.** **Seen:** Medical v2 planned cards (~110px for one line), past cards with empty bottoms. **Rule:** Cards size to their content. No `min-height` or fixed `height` on content cards; equal-height rows come from `align-items: stretch` on the row. Put metadata such as a countdown on the title row, not on its own line. **Check:** state a max px height per card type in the brief and measure it.

**L3 · Sticky side panels that fight the layout.** **Seen:** Goals Hammond panel #495. **Rule:** No `position: sticky` side columns unless the brief specifies one. **Check:** scroll the full page at 1440 and 390; nothing overlaps or detaches.

**L4 · Rail or background stops partway down the page.** **Seen:** Medical v2 full-page capture (rail ends at ~690px). **Rule:** The rail is fixed/sticky with `height: 100dvh`; the page background sits on `body`. **Check:** full-page screenshot, then scroll to the bottom.

**L5 · Toolbar wraps into a pile on phone.** **Seen:** Medical v2 at 390 (4 rows of controls). **Rule:** At < 720px a toolbar is search + one **Filters** button (a sheet) + an icon primary action. **Check:** the toolbar is ≤ 2 rows at 390.

**L6 · Chip text wraps inside a rounded pill.** **Seen:** Organisations wall #508 (the NSW HALT "Member" chip wrapped to 3 centred lines). **Rule:** A chip is one line: `white-space: nowrap`, the detail truncates with an ellipsis at `max-width: 100%`, and the full text goes in `title`. Long detail belongs on the detail page, not the tile. **Check:** every chip has `getClientRects().length === 1` at 390 and 1440.

**L7 · Week lock titles spill across day columns.** **Seen:** Tasks Almanac/Week chrome “This week's locks” (Friday title over SAT/SUN for ~30s while calendar sources stalled); Life Calendar first open held paint for forced `refresh` + `/api/calendar-ghosts` so only the Schedule chip showed for a long settle (2026-09); Professional organisation page (2026-10-02): directory → chart → Ann → opportunities → timeline awaited one after another, and the chart GET reloaded the whole structure once per person (10.9–17.9s), so everything below the chart waited on it. **Rule:** A 7-column locks grid uses `minmax(0, 1fr)` tracks; each `.calendar-lock-row` is a grid item with `min-width: 0`, `overflow: hidden`, and the task text wraps/clamps *inside* its day cell (`line-clamp`, `white-space: normal`). Full title in `title`. Do not “fix” by single-line ellipsis alone while the cell can still grow. First calendar paint must not wait on every foreign hub source, fonts, serial Blob walks, or a full Life `refresh`+ghosts chain — skeleton / Tideline within ~1s, then progressive sources. **Check:** at 1440 a long Friday title’s `getBoundingClientRect().right` stays left of the Saturday cell’s left; Almanac shows `data-part="almanac-loading"` (or settled chart) within 2s of navigation; Life `#/calendar` paints `.hub-calendar` / Tideline (or `data-part="calendar-loading"`) before forced refresh settles — never Schedule-only blank for tens of seconds.

**L8 · Flex-end slot clips tall content at the top.** **Seen:** Knowledge Thinking Mirror Council Turn 5 / synthesis (2026-09) — summary sat outside `.protocol-turn-card-slot`, slot used `justify-content:flex-end` + `overflow:hidden`, so long synthesis cut the first line at the card top; controller speaker had no portrait chrome; scrubber current-dot was muted so Turn 5 header looked out of sync with the dots. **Rule:** Put every card that belongs to the turn stack (including run summary) inside the scroll slot. Prefer `justify-content:safe flex-end` (or flex-start + spacer) and `overflow-y:auto` on the slot — never `flex-end` + `overflow:hidden` for variable-height reading content. Controller / synthesis turns get visible council chrome. Header turn index and scrubber `is-current` share one `viewingIndex`. **Check:** completed Mirror fixture at 1280 and 390 shows full first line of synthesis, `.protocol-portrait--council` present, summary inside `.protocol-turn-card-slot`, and `data-protocol-scrub-index` matches `Turn N of N`.

**L9 · Height-locked rail clips destinations with no scroll.** **Seen:** Knowledge Notebooks (2026-09) — sticky `.rail` at `height: 100dvh` with `.rail__nav { flex: 0 0 auto }` and no `overflow-y`, and `hubSwitcherHtml("knowledge")` rendered **after** `</nav>`, so HUBS → **Professional** sat outside the scrollport and was cut off. Professional/Tasks already mount the switcher inside `railNav` and scroll `.hub-rail__nav`; kit `rail.css` lacked the overflow contract. **Rule:** A viewport-pinned rail (`100dvh`, sticky/fixed) keeps brand outside the scroll and puts `flex: 1 1 0; min-height: 0; overflow-y: auto` on `.hub-rail__nav` (kit) / hub nav aliases. Mount the hub switcher **inside** that nav (`appendHubSwitcher(hubSwitcherHost(railNav), …)` or equivalent). Never `flex: 0 0 auto` on that nav when the rail is height-locked, and never leave `.hub-rail__hubs` as a sibling below a height-locked overflow-hidden rail. **Check:** at desktop ~1280 with a full HUBS list, `nav.contains(hubs)` is true; scroll `.hub-rail__nav` / `.rail__nav` until Professional’s full row is inside the rail’s client rect (`getBoundingClientRect`); `scrollHeight > clientHeight` when content exceeds the rail.

**L10 · Strip appended inside a fixed-height box it was styled to sit under.** **Seen:** Travel trip page (2026-09): the `.daybar` (bottom-rounded, own background) was appended *inside* `.scene` (`aspect-ratio`, `max-height`, `overflow:hidden`), so the day chips overprinted the city title on every trip. **Rule:** A strip whose CSS rounds only its bottom corners is a sibling after the card, not a child. Never append flow content into an `overflow:hidden` art box sized by `aspect-ratio`. **Check:** at 1440 and 390 the strip's `getBoundingClientRect().top` ≥ the scene's `bottom`, and the title's box intersects no chip.

**L11 · Children appended outside the padded grid their CSS targets.** **Seen:** Travel ticket card (2026-09): `.ticket` has `padding: 0` and `.ticket .body` is the padded 3-column grid, but the depart/arrive `.hm` spans and Edit were appended to the card, so times and title ran flush against the card edges; the arrival guide `details.landing` lacked `.card` (no padding/radius) and its summary spread label / bare text node / marker with `space-between`. Timeline rows (`.go` hop, `.checkin`, `.pen`) started at x=0 while cards and `.add-row` sat at the 46px card column. **Rule:** Before shipping a component, dump its DOM next to its CSS selectors: every child the CSS positions (`.body .hm`, grid columns) must actually be inside that parent. Siblings in a timeline list share one left edge. **Check:** at 1440 and 390, every text node's `getBoundingClientRect().left` is ≥ its card's left + padding; all `.list` children except `.stop` rails share the `.add-row` left ±1px.

**L12 · Trailing chrome shifts a “centred” title off its visual anchor.** **Seen:** Tasks Day Dial face bar (2026-10): `‹ Dress watch ›` sat in a flex row with `Auto` after the next arrow, so the title’s centre was ~28px left of the dial; phone zoom-bar `order: -1` on `.dd__round` also stacked both arrows before the name inside a nav. **Rule:** When a label must line up with a centred visual (dial, hero, mark), put the label (and any flanking controls that belong with it) in a true centre track (`1fr auto 1fr` or absolute centre). Trailing actions sit in the side track and must not share the title’s flex grow. Reset `order` on nested controls that inherit a global order rule. **Check:** at 390 and 1280, the face title’s horizontal centre is within ±2px of `[data-part="watch"]`; `apps/tasks/tests/unit/day-dial-face-bar.test.ts` stays green.

## S: Surfaces and CSS scope

**S1 · Styles scoped to another page's container.** **Seen:** Medical v2 mini lab panel. The bloods meter CSS was scoped to `#body-bloods-dashboard`, so the reused `markerRow()` rendered as floating black dots. **Rule:** When you reuse a component somewhere new, grep its CSS for ID/parent-scoped selectors and rescope them to the component class. **Check:** screenshot the reused component in its new home next to its original home; they match.

**S2 · Utility class that doesn't exist in this hub.** **Seen:** Tasks `glass-tile` no-op #476. **Rule:** Give each card an explicit surface: `background: var(--glass)` or `var(--paper)`, `border: 1px solid var(--line)`, `border-radius`, `box-shadow: var(--elev-1)`. **Check:** the computed `background-color` isn't `transparent`.

**S3 · See-through sheets and dialogs.** **Seen:** Tasks Programs sheet #472. **Rule:** Sheets and popovers use `background: var(--paper)`, `backdrop-filter: none`. **Check:** no underlying text is visible through an open sheet.

**S4 · Shell class with no CSS in this hub.** **Seen:** Travel `/travel/` (2026-09): `.skip-link` had no rule in Travel (Professional/Tasks define it in their own `hub.css`), so a raw "Skip to content" line sat above the rail; `.hub-rail__back` had no rule anywhere, so "← Life" ran into the brand as "LifeLIFE HUB · TRAVEL". Professional comm + meeting pages (30/09/26): `.clare`, `.clare__who`, `.clare-row`, `.draft`, `.muted` were emitted by `comm-page.ts` / `meeting-page.ts` but defined in no stylesheet, so every Clare card rendered as raw text. **Rule:** When you copy a shell from another hub, copy the hub-local CSS it depends on too; every class the shell emits is defined somewhere this hub loads. **Check:** for each class in `shell.ts`, `grep` finds a rule in a stylesheet this app imports; `.skip-link`'s bounding box is off-screen at rest.

**S4 · SVG falling back to default black fill.** **Seen:** Medical v2 meter dots; Day dial work-block and study arcs (29/09/26): `.dd-arc` had fills for teaching, professional, health and Corey only, so every Tasks work block drew solid black on the dial and the mini week dials. **Rule:** Every SVG shape gets an explicit `fill`/`stroke` via class or attribute. **Check:** no pure `#000` in the computed fills of the new SVG.

**S5 · Dropdown painted under the next section.** **Seen:** Teaching lesson editor (Codex run 10, 05/10/26): the context bar has `backdrop-filter` (its own stacking context) and no `z-index`, so the canvas below painted over the Lesson options menu; clicking History hit the Connections section instead and "did nothing". **Rule:** Any bar that owns a dropdown or popover and has `backdrop-filter`, `transform`, `filter` or `opacity < 1` also gets `position: relative` and a `z-index` above the content it overlaps. **Check:** open every menu in the bar at 1440 and run `document.elementFromPoint` on the centre of each item; it returns the item, not something underneath.

**S6 · Phone touch-target rule widened every chip.** **Seen:** Tasks Board domain pills (2026-10): Goals mobile CSS listed bare `.hub-chip { min-width: 2.75rem }`, so short Board labels ("Life", "Other") sat in 44px capsules with left-aligned text while longer pills ("Teaching") looked fine. **Rule:** A 44px touch-target rule scopes to the surface that needs it (Goals sheets / runway / check-in), never a bare kit class that Board cards reuse. Pill chips use `justify-content: center`, and densified Board chips reset `min-width: 0`. **Check:** at 390, Board `.hub-chip[data-area="life"]` has equal left/right content gaps (±1px) and computed `min-width` is `0px`; `apps/tasks/tests/unit/chip-centering.test.ts` stays green.

## V: Visibility and state

**V1 · `[hidden]` doesn't hide.** **Seen:** Goals Direction editor (G-18). **Rule:** Any element toggled with `hidden` that has a `display:` rule also gets `.x[hidden]{display:none}`. **Check:** after hiding, `offsetHeight === 0`.

**V2 · Controls disagree with the view.** **Seen:** Goals Term/Year pills (G-17). **Rule:** One state object drives both the view and the control's active class, recomputed on every render. **Check:** change the view by a *different* input (pinch, keyboard, URL) and the pill follows.

**V3 · Duplicate controls for the same concept.** **Seen:** Medical v2, two Weeks/Months/Years pill sets on screen; Professional Home (2026-09) — `+ Log PD event`, Year at a glance, and Accreditation progress each rendered twice when boot set `location.hash = '#/home'` (hashchange + `await paint()`) while Home appended after an await with no `isCurrent` guard; Knowledge Thinking (2026-09) — every `render()` remounted `.protocols-root` and catalog/past-runs settles wiped the library so `protocol-deal` replayed from opacity 0 (load flash); Travel `/travel/` (2026-09) — single-trip auto-redirect did `location.hash = '#/trip/…'` and returned with an empty canvas while hashchange re-painted; Professional organisation page (2026-10-02): two **Edit chart** buttons (header and How card) opening the same editor; Organisations wall search (2026-10-02): `writeHash` assigned `location.hash` on every debounced keystroke (and every sort/group/filter change), so the router repainted the whole page — refetching the ~5s directory and rebuilding the search box — once per letter. **Rule:** Each view has one zoom/density control. Controls for a collapsed region hide with it. Async route paints must pass `isCurrent` (or equivalent generation) and commit the canvas in one replace — never clear, await, then append on a shared host. Default hash via `history.replaceState`, not `location.hash =`, when a hashchange listener also paints. Keep mounted surfaces (Thinking library, Tideline) across settle paints; skip entrance animation on remount / patch past-runs in place. **Check:** list every control on the screen; no two share labels. For Home: `document.querySelectorAll('.pro-home__actions, .pro-home__yearstrip, [data-part="accreditation-progress"]')` counts are 1/1/1 after load from `/professional/` with empty hash. For Thinking: after navigate-to / past-runs settle, the same eight `.protocol-card` nodes stay mounted (or `.protocol-library.is-settled` disables deal); no blank→deal flash on catalog/past-runs. For Travel: signed-in single-trip boot paints trip UI in the same `paint()` turn (no empty `.hub-canvas__body` between list and trip).

**V4 · Different parts of the page disagree.** **Seen:** Medical v2: the episode card said "day 4", the band said "2 notes", and a loose minor line showed the third note; the strip said "2 planned" while Next showed 3. **Rule:** Derive summary counts and cards from **one** model function, never recompute them in the renderer. **Check:** a test builds the page model once and asserts every summary figure against the same source.

**V5 · Save failure hides the reason.** **Seen:** Travel add-item sheet (2026-09): `catch {}` turned every server error (409 version conflict, missing ticket fields, bad link) into "Could not save. Try again." After a conflict, trying again could never succeed, so about 10 minutes of entries were lost. **Rule:** A save handler shows the server's message and highlights the field it names. It retries a 409 once against a freshly fetched version, checks the server's required fields before sending, and never drops typed input without saying so. **Check:** unit tests for a 409 followed by success, a validation error whose text reaches the UI with the form kept open, and each required field refused on the client.

**V6 · An async close callback removes the next sheet.** **Seen:** Professional org chart **Units & members…** (2026-10-02, 3/3 in the live test): the button called the editor's `close()`, which flushes layout and then calls `onClose` a tick later, and then opened the structure sheet. `onClose` did `editorSheet?.remove()` on the page's shared "current sheet" variable, which by then pointed at the new sheet, so the only way to add a faculty vanished on open. **Rule:** Each sheet's callbacks capture and act on *that* sheet (`const sheet = open…(); onClose: () => closeSheet(sheet)`), never on a shared "current" variable. Any `close()` that awaits must not be followed synchronously by opening its replacement unless the callbacks are sheet-scoped. **Check:** a unit test clicks the hand-off control, drains microtasks and a macrotask, and asserts the new sheet is still in the DOM and the old one is gone.

**V7 · A repaint resets the view the user chose.** **Seen:** Professional org page (2026-10-02): view (Outline/Flow/People), zoom and collapsed containers were local to the section render, so Done in the editor, or collapsing a faculty, snapped back to Flow at 100% with everything expanded. **Rule:** View state the user sets (tab, zoom, collapsed groups, filters) lives outside the function that repaints the section and is reapplied on every repaint. **Check:** set Outline, zoom twice, collapse a group, trigger the repaint path (save in the editor), and assert all three survived.

**V8 · Two renderers for the same data.** **Seen:** Professional org chart (2026-10-02): Edit chart drew boxes at your dragged positions with faculties as a grey text line inside a box, while the organisation page re-ran ELK auto-layout on every load, ignored the saved positions, drew faculties as containers (including empty ones the editor never showed), styled "works with" the same as "reports to" and added inferred lines. Compare used the ELK view too, and Outline listed faculty members from a different link type, so a box put in a faculty in the editor vanished from Outline. Adam: "the org chart preview on the organisation page is totally, totally different". **Rule:** One model and one drawing function per kind of chart; the editor is that drawing plus interaction, and every read-only view (page, compare, outline) is built from the same model. Never let a second view re-derive layout, grouping or line semantics. **Check:** a test paints the shared model read-only and asserts the same containers, boxes and line classes the editor paints; a screenshot of the page chart and the editor at 1440 shows identical positions.

**V9 · Busy button and status line show the same rotating copy.** **Seen:** Knowledge Chat / From a book (2026-10): submit and `.chat__status` both bound to `waitLine`, so "Chasing the named source…" appeared twice — once in the primary button (right) and once as grey text (left, next row). **Rule:** While an agent turn is in flight the primary button stays a static label (`thinking`); only `.chat__status` shows the rotating wait line. Put status in `.hub-ai-bar__aside` with the button so they share one row. **Check:** during a mocked busy turn, `button[type=submit]` text is `thinking`, `.hub-ai-bar__aside .chat__status` holds the wait line, and both share the aside's vertical centre ±2px at 390 and 1440.

**V10 · A test screen labels the answer.** **Seen:** Life City glance #740 (2026-10-08): the day caption read "Suspended service", "Deleted yesterday" and "No check-in morning", which are the answers the glance test asks Adam to give, and the reminder strip and chat bubble from the hub shell sat over the test. **Rule:** A screen built to test whether a picture communicates shows no text that names what the picture is meant to say. Give it a test mode with neutral labels ("Day A") and no hub chrome. **Check:** in test mode, `document.body.innerText` contains none of the golden day names or any snapshot reason text until a hover or click.

## R: Responsive

**R1 · Phone DOM at desktop, or desktop DOM at phone.** **Seen:** Goals runway (G-43). **Rule:** When JS renders different DOM per breakpoint, re-render via a `matchMedia` listener; CSS alone isn't enough. **Check:** resize 390 → 1440 → 390 without reloading.

**R2 · Horizontal page scroll on phone.** **Rule:** Wide content (charts, strips) scrolls inside its own `overflow-x:auto` box. **Check:** `document.documentElement.scrollWidth === innerWidth` at 390.

**R3 · A component that works on desktop but breaks at 390.** **Seen:** Medical v2 bloods rows (dot, value and status each on separate lines); Teaching Trash at 390 (Codex run 10, 05/10/26): a 4-column table ~792px wide with `display:flex` on the actions `<td>`, so Restore / Delete permanently sat off-screen and could not be tapped. **Rule:** The brief gives the phone layout explicitly (e.g. "2 rows: label+value / bar+status"). **Check:** a 390 screenshot of every new component, not just the page top.

**R4 · Form sheet zooms and clips on iPhone / untappable bottom actions.** **Hard gate:** any sheet, dialog, modal, or long form with Save/Add/Cancel/Confirm/Remove — check this every time (also pinned in `AGENTS.md` and `.cursor/rules/ui-failure-register.mdc`). **Seen:** Travel add/edit item sheet (2026-09): inputs at 14px made iOS Safari zoom in on focus and stay zoomed, and the sheet was an 85vh floating card with 16px padding, so Save/Cancel/Remove sat below the visible viewport; amount and currency wrapped onto two full-width rows. **Seen again:** Travel flight add sheet (2026-10): Add/Cancel were ~13px-font pills stuck on the home-indicator with sticky-in-scroll, so they were untappable after filling a long form — Adam lost the entry. **Seen again:** Creatine intake/routine Confirm cards (10/10/26): shared phone actions were 36px (18px for Show all & edit), and long expanded notes buried the action row after the viewport shortened. Shared actions now use 44px token sizing and expanded fields scroll within a capped region; real 390px tests edit long notes and click visible Confirm/Discard without scrolling to them. **Rule:** Every `input`, `select` and `textarea` has a computed `font-size` of at least 16px below 720px. On phones a form sheet is a bottom sheet: full width, flush to the bottom, `max-height` in `dvh`, with a **docked** (not scroll-sticky) action row whose buttons are ≥ 44px tall, share the row width, and are padded by `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`. Short paired controls (amount + currency, search + button) stay on one row. **Check:** at 390, every form control's computed `font-size` is ≥ 16px; `[data-part="form-actions"]` (or the sheet's action row) stays in view with `getBoundingClientRect().bottom` ≤ `innerHeight` and each action `.btn` `height` ≥ 44; after scrolling fields to the bottom the actions stay docked; `scrollWidth === innerWidth`.

**R5 · Phone hides a control and nothing replaces it.** **Seen:** Calendar zoom pills (30/09/26): Week and Day set `[data-part="zoom-pills"]{display:none}` below 720px and the Almanac hid its pills too, so on a phone you were stuck on Week with no way to reach Day, Term, Year or Almanac. Term/Year kept theirs but hid ‹ › and Today, and each view placed Zoom at a different height. **Rule:** Never `display:none` a navigation control at a breakpoint unless the same PR adds its phone form (a sheet, a menu, a full-width row). A control shared by sibling views sits at the same place in every one of them on the phone. **Check:** at 390, every zoom stop is reachable by tapping from every other, and `[data-part="zoom-pills"]`'s `getBoundingClientRect()` is identical ±1px across all views.

**R6 · Bottom sheet opens under the fixed tab bar.** **Seen:** Tideline item card at 390 (30/09/26): the card was `position:fixed; bottom:12px` with the same `z-index` as `.hub-mobile-nav`, so only its title row showed above the tab bar. **Rule:** A phone sheet that isn't full-screen sits above the bottom bar: `bottom: calc(4.65rem + env(safe-area-inset-bottom) + gap)` with a higher `z-index`, and a `max-height` in `dvh` that leaves room for it. **Check:** at 390 with the sheet open, its `getBoundingClientRect().bottom` is ≤ the tab bar's `top`.

## D: Data shown truthfully

**D1 · Made-up precision.** **Seen:** Medical v2 "to book" MRCP shown as 26/09/26 and month-precision colonoscopy as 01/02/27. **Rule:** Render what the data knows: a day, a month ("Feb 2027"), "To book", or "~22/10/26" for an estimate. Never substitute today or the 1st of the month. **Check:** a fixture per precision level asserts the rendered label.

**D2 · Item placed by the wrong date.** **Seen:** Medical v2 active episode positioned at its first entry, not its latest. **Rule:** The brief states the sort key of every grouped item. **Check:** a model test asserts the order.

**D3 · Screenshots taken on demo data.** **Seen:** Medical v2 (demo rail, river ending at Aug 2026, missing records). **Rule:** Acceptance screenshots use real data on the live umbrella or a production-data copy. **Check:** the screenshots contain named real records the brief lists.

**D4 · Mapping tables that miss real values.** **Seen:** Medical v2 Mind lane missing although therapy and ADHD visits exist. **Rule:** Test a keyword/lane map against real titles and providers from the store, not invented ones. **Check:** one test per lane using real record titles.

**D6 · Derived metric computed from partial input.** **Seen:** Organisations #508. Warmth was computed from only the person→organisation link (no meetings, comms or events), so everyone banded "cold" and every warmth bar was the same grey. That was a second warmth calculation disagreeing with People's. Also, import `created_at` timestamps were used as "when you got to know them". **Rule:** A derived value (warmth, a first-contact date, a count) comes from the one function and the full input the owning page uses. An import timestamp is never an event date. **Check:** a test compares the value against the owning model for the same ids, and the ledger records the real distribution (e.g. "12 warm / 9 cooling / 35 cold"). A degenerate distribution (all one value) is investigated before shipping.

**D5 · Ugly or ambiguous derived text.** **Seen:** "38 in" chip; "Ordered 24 Sep" with no subject; dates not in dd/mm/yy. **Rule:** Every generated label reads as a phrase a person would write; dates use `formatDisplayDate` or relative text. **Check:** read every string in the screenshot aloud.

**D7 · Relationship read from a field that is always the same value.** **Seen:** Travel world map (2026-09): legs were built from each ticket's `city_id → arrive_city_id`, but tickets are filed under their arrival city so both were equal; every booked leg was zero-length and the map fell back to dotted "not booked" gaps, with no Sydney start. **Rule:** Before drawing an edge from two fields, check them on the real data file: if they are equal for every row, derive the missing end from order (previous stop / home). **Check:** a leg-builder test on the real trip shape asserts SYD→KUL and KUL→IST are `booked` and non-zero length.

**D8 · Lookup miss throws away the label the source already had.** **Seen:** Professional meeting room (30/09/26): an attendee whose ref was a Blob copy of an imported student missed the People directory (colleagues only; `students` ignored), and the card printed "Unknown person · New to you" although the link carried "Rohan Arianayagam". **Rule:** When a join misses, fall back to the label on the record you joined from, and look in every list the directory returns. Never claim a fact ("New to you") from a miss. **Check:** a test with an attendee ref absent from the directory renders the link's name and no "New to you".

**D9 · Picker rows that can't be told apart.** **Seen:** Professional new-meeting attendee picker (30/09/26): "@rohan" listed Rohan, Rohan, Rohan Arianayagam, Rohan Arianayagam, each sub-labelled only "person" (Blob copies of two imported students). **Rule:** Every picker row carries a distinguishing supporting line (student / job title · organisation / "Not in People yet"). Copies that resolve to the same person collapse to one row, preferring the ref the directory knows. **Check:** a unit test with same-name hits asserts one row per real person and distinct supporting labels.

**D10 · Calendar chip with no context.** **Seen:** Professional Tideline (30/09/26): meeting chips showed the title only (time faded out on short chips); the projection carried no location or attendees. **Rule:** A meeting/comm chip's meta is `time · with <names> · <place>`, built from the projection, and the chip's `title` attribute carries the full line so short chips still reveal it on hover. **Check:** a model test asserts the chip meta for a meeting with an attendee and a location.

**D11 · Planned time with no place on the time axis, and status nobody can change.** **Seen:** Life calendar, #676 (04/10/26): tasks Adam had time-blocked (HSC feedback emails at 3:00, 3:15, 3:30…) sat in "Due · all day" on Week and never reached the Day Dial. Clare had written the start into `due_time`, which the schema defines as a deadline. Ticking one off removed it from every view, and no calendar view (Dial, Linear, Week, Term) could tick or change the status of anything. **Rule:** `due_time` is a deadline only.
