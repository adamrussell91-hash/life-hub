The MegaKit free Standard archive was downloaded from the publisher's itch.io
page, https://quaternius.itch.io/stylized-nature-megakit. Its bundled licence
explicitly says CC0. The paid Pro and Source editions were not downloaded.
The animal files and licence came from the public Drive folder linked by
Quaternius's animal-pack page. Kenney source files were already in this repo;
their bundled licence and current publisher page both identify CC0.

Grove modifications: sizes and pivots normalised; metallic materials removed;
selected tree trunks made pale and selected foliage made pink; prop foliage
and wood colours adjusted; six flower petal colours; animations renamed and
restricted to idle/walk/run/eat; geometry compressed with Meshopt; necessary
alpha-mask foliage textures converted to WebP at no more than 512 px.
Source geometry is credited per model in `manifest.json`. Recoloured birch and
blossom stand-ins and scaled growth-stage fallbacks are explicitly identified
there; they are not claimed to be new botanical or growth-stage meshes.
