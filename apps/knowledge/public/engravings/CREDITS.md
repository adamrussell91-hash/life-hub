# Engravings

Cut from Olaus Magnus, *Carta Marina* (Venice, 1539), as reproduced in the
black-and-white facsimile plate in F. W. Lucas, *The Annals of the Voyages of the
Brothers Nicolò and Antonio Zeno* (London, 1898), plate 4. Public domain.
Source scan: Wikimedia Commons, "Lucas 1898 Plate 4 Olaus Magnus Carta Marina 1539
NW Portion".

Each figure has two masks: `<name>.png` (the ink) and `<name>-fill.png` (its
parchment silhouette). The map colours both, so they work by day and by night.
