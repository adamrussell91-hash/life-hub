Early-twenties male, six feet four inches tall, physically imposing and socially overconfident. His voice immediately suggests a very tall, heavily built young man.

Accent: Unmistakably stereotypical Southern Californian college-frat gym bro. Strong West Coast dude-bro cadence, relaxed vowels, softened consonants, frequent bro and dude emphasis, and confident upward inflections. Immediately Californian and frat-house, without becoming a cartoon surfer. Never Australian.

Pitch: An unusually deep natural young bass-baritone, centered around 75 to 90 Hz in relaxed speech. Keep the baseline low, heavy and substantial. Excited phrases can rise but retain deep vocal weight; never bright, thin, light or tenor-like. Clearly early twenties, without an artificial growl or elderly action-hero sound.

Timbre and resonance: Dark, warm, muscular and slightly raspy, with the acoustic size of a six-foot-four man. Mild vocal fry starts relaxed sentences; hype becomes cleaner and more projected while staying dark and deep. Strong low chest resonance, substantial lower harmonics and forward placement. Naturally loud, without throat compression, theatrical booming or gravelly affectation.

Pace: Very fast and impulsive, around 195 to 215 words per minute, as though his mouth started before his brain approved the sentence. Keep punchlines and safety cues intelligible as phrases tumble together.

Dynamics: Animated conversation punctuated by explosive gym-floor shouts. Inappropriate or accidentally revealing asides suddenly drop into a softer, close, breathy register, then snap back into loud masculine bravado. Audible breaths, small laughs and occasional excited voice cracks. Physically involved in the training session, never reading booth copy.

Emotion: Peak stereotypical frat-bro energy: cocky, loud, impulsive and at home in a Southern California college weight room. Earnest encouragement hides beneath crude, flirtatious locker-room humour. Delighted by progress, overconfident and confused by his intense admiration for his bro's physique.
