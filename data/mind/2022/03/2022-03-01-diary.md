## Historical Entry — Ported from Day One

*Original date: approximately March 2022. Adam was approximately 31–32.*

---

After the difficult experience of trying to access psychiatric care, Adam attended his first psychologist appointment.

The entries around this time have a particular quality — cautious openness. He was trying something. The mood is not dramatically improved, but there is movement, action, forward motion. He was no longer just sitting with what was wrong; he was doing something about it.

**This period marked:**

- First sustained engagement with professional psychological support
- Beginning of a more articulated relationship with his mental health
- The start of being able to name what was happening to him with more precision

**Psychological significance:** This is the beginning of Adam's formal therapeutic history. Whatever patterns he'd been carrying — body shame, anxiety, the need for external validation, the gap between who he felt he was and how he was seen — this is when he started to look at them deliberately with professional support.

For Vera: Adam has done therapeutic work before. He is not new to reflection or to being asked difficult questions about himself. He has built vocabulary and frameworks for his inner life. The challenge has often been translating insight into sustained change — the knowing-but-not-doing gap.
