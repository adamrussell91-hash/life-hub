## Historical Entry — Ported from Day One

*Original date: 10 August 2022. Adam was 31–32. He and Corey had been together for 10 years.*

---

Adam and Corey celebrated their 10th anniversary. Corey gave Adam a Le Creuset — a significant, considered, expensive kitchen gift — and Adam cried.

The tears weren't about the object. They were about what the object meant: being known. Corey knew Adam well enough to give him something that would genuinely delight him — something that connected to cooking, to home, to the pleasure of the domestic. It was an act of precise love.

The entry captures the particular emotion of being seen clearly by the person you love and having that seeing made tangible.

**Psychological significance:** This entry is important for multiple reasons:

1. It shows that after a difficult early 2022 (psychiatric care attempts, beginning therapy), August brought a moment of profound emotional connection
2. It documents Adam's capacity for deep emotional response to acts of care and being known
3. It establishes that the relationship with Corey, even after 10 years, was capable of producing genuine, uncomplicated gratitude and love
4. The Le Creuset itself became a household object with emotional weight

For Vera: Adam is deeply moved by being known and seen, especially by Corey. Gifts and gestures that demonstrate attentiveness carry enormous meaning for him. This is a love language that matters.
