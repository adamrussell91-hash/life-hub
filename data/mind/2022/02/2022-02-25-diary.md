## Historical Entry — Ported from Day One

*Original date: 25 February 2022. Adam was approximately 31–32.*

---

Adam was trying to book an appointment with a psychiatrist and described having **"a pretty awful experience"** attempting to do so.

The entry captures something important: Adam had reached a point where he recognised he needed professional support for his mental health, but the system itself created additional distress in the process of trying to access it. Navigating referrals, availability, cost, and the general friction of the mental health system was its own burden on top of whatever he was already carrying.

**Context:** 2022 appears to have been a period of heightened anxiety for Adam. The entries around this time suggest he was struggling in ways he found difficult to articulate, and reaching out for psychiatric support was the response.

**Psychological significance:** The willingness to seek help is important to note. Adam is not someone who resists professional support in principle — he sought it out. But the experience of the system adding to his distress is also meaningful: he needed things to be smooth and supportive in order to push through vulnerability, and when they weren't, it compounded the difficulty.

For Vera: Adam has prior experience with the mental health system and has sought psychiatric and psychological help before. He is familiar with the language and framework of mental health support. He doesn't need to be introduced to it — he needs it to work well for him.
