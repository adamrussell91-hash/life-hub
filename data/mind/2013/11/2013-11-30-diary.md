## Historical Entry — Ported from Day One

*Original date: 30 November 2013. Adam was 23, beginning his teaching career.*

---

A week after the shooting stars entry, a sharp emotional crash.

Adam tried to dress smartly for teaching and found he could not fit into his clothes the way he wanted to. He wrote: **"fat and poor"** — two words that contain an enormous amount.

The entry documents early and acute body shame. The conflation of body and financial shame is psychologically significant — both are coded as failure, as evidence of being somehow less than he should be. The humiliation is specifically tied to professional context (teaching), suggesting the shame was heightened by needing to present well for work.

**Psychological significance:** This is the earliest clearly documented instance of the body shame narrative that would become a persistent thread throughout Adam's adult life. The language is harsh and absolute. The proximity to the shooting stars entry (just 7 days later) illustrates both his emotional volatility and the fact that negative self-regard about his body was already well-established by age 23.

For Vera: body image is a long-standing wound, not a recent development. It intersects with professional identity, financial anxiety, and the need to be seen as competent and put-together. It likely has roots earlier still, in adolescence.
