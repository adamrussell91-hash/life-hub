## Historical Entry — Ported from Day One

*Original date: 23 November 2013. Adam was 23. He and Corey had been together approximately 1.5 years.*

---

One of the most purely joyful entries in the entire journal archive.

Adam and Corey watched the Doctor Who 50th anniversary special together — a significant cultural moment for both of them as fans. Afterwards, they walked to the Stockton bridge and saw shooting stars.

The entry is written with uncomplicated happiness. No anxiety, no self-analysis, no sense of threat. Just two people sharing something they loved and being rewarded by the universe with something beautiful.

**Psychological significance:** This is a foundational positive memory in Adam and Corey's relationship. It represents the kind of effortless shared joy that Adam would later grieve the loss of, particularly during the 2025 separation crisis. When Adam writes in 2025 "I just wish I had Corey," this is partly what he means — this version of their life together.

For Vera: Adam has experienced genuine, uncomplicated partnership happiness. The 2025 pain is loss, not absence of capacity for joy. This memory is evidence of that capacity.
