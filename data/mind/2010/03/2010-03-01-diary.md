## Historical Entry — Ported from Day One

*Period: early 2010. Adam was 19–20, living in Newcastle, studying at university.*

---

The university Newcastle period captures Adam at his most socially alive and unguarded. Entries from this time show someone who was curious, funny, impulsive, and genuinely hopeful — capable of sudden joy and deeply invested in friendships.

He wrote with immediacy and humour. He documented small events — nights out, conversations, the texture of student life — with a warmth that reveals someone who wanted connection and found it, even if temporarily.

**Key character traits visible in this period:**

- Impulsivity that he both enjoyed and was slightly embarrassed by
- Strong social attunement — very aware of group dynamics
- Intellectual identity already forming (clear sense that he was "a writer" or "creative")
- Optimism coexisting with anxiety about the future

**Psychological significance:** This is Adam before sustained disappointment. The contrast with 2025 entries is stark — the playfulness and spontaneous connection visible here had become much harder to access by his mid-30s. Understanding this baseline helps contextualise how much the intervening years cost him in terms of ease and openness.
