## Historical Entry — Ported from Day One

*Original date: 17 February 2010. Adam was 19 years old.*

---

First documented emotional crisis in the journal record. Adam wrote: **"I feel like my life is crumbling around me."**

He was working a retail job that felt beneath him and directionless. Financial pressure was present. There was a strong sense of stagnation — life not matching inner expectations — combined with social isolation and the particular despair of a bright young person in an environment that didn't fit.

This entry marks the beginning of a long-running pattern: high expectations for himself, frustration when reality doesn't match, and the urge to name what's happening internally even when he can't yet act on it.

**Psychological significance:** First recorded instance of existential crisis framing. The language ("crumbling") suggests all-or-nothing thinking and catastrophising that would resurface throughout Adam's life, particularly in 2025. This is the origin point of his relationship with documenting emotional states.
