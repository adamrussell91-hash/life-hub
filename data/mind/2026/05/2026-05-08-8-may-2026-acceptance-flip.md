Arrived carrying a concentrated work load with multiple high stakes commitments clustered into one week and a sense of being close to snapping. The birthday night collapsed under obligations, delays and unshared planning which sharpened resentment about relational labour at home and health consequences of last minute food. Ran an acceptance based exercise to locate frustration in the body, describe its physical qualities, step back as observer and make room for it, then add warmth and kindness via a hand on the body. Struggle rating reduced to three and frustration flipped into determination, leaving a noticeably steadier and more workable internal stance heading into next week.
