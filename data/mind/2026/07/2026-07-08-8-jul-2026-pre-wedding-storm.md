What was brought today was the wedding — and everything that had collected around it.

The practical logistics had resolved themselves in the forty-eight hours before the session: venue confirmed, guitarist confirmed, outfit sorted after five shopping centres and a surprisingly good result at H&M. What hadn't resolved was underneath all of it. Wendy. The panic attack in the car the previous Saturday. The medication incident with Corey, still carrying a residual 2% that attaches to small irritations. The biologic infusion four days earlier, with its full catalogue of side effects running through an already depleted system.

Kate named it clearly: a perfect storm. That framing was useful. Not because it explained everything away, but because it gave the weight somewhere accurate to land.

The choice point work was the centre of the session — mapping the hooked thoughts (betrayal, "it'll never change", "this is the rest of my life"), the away moves (dismissing or rationalising feelings, becoming snappy, then over-apologising), and the values he actually wants to bring: kindness, self-compassion, attentiveness, bravery. The towards moves were concrete and small — giving Corey space without expectation, carving out intentional pauses for himself.

The grief around Wendy was present but didn't get its own unhurried space. It blended with everything else. The cathartic release expected in March, in Queensland, never came. What's arriving now may be that.

Next session: 25 July (9am). The wedding will be done.
