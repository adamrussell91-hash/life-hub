Had a ticket, was genuinely looking forward to it, and instead i was horizontal on the couch building an HTML timeline app in Claude because apparently even when i'm dying i can't stop making things for school. We've both been too tired and too miserable to even talk much. Just two sick people coexisting in tired silence, occasionally handing each other food. It's fine.

Going back to work tomorrow though and i am really not looking forward to it. Up early, push through, pretend i'm fine. Year 11 and 12 marking is about to land within the next couple of weeks too. I just want a holiday. That's not a dramatic statement. I just genuinely wish i could stop for a bit.

Everything's travelling along with this odd calm unease. No emergencies, no big dramas, no storms on the horizon. But the sun's not shining very brightly either. It's just grey and flat and tired. Like me, basically.
