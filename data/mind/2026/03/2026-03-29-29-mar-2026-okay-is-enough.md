Arrived post-Penelope session having recounted the full week (Tue 24 to Sun 29 Mar). Mood data averaged 4.3/10 all week. Energy Low every day. Presented as flat but stable.

Opened by testing whether his Sunday "okay" was genuine acceptance or resignation from depleted expectations. He identified it as the former: still here, still moving forward, things slid off the plate but he is putting them back. Believable read.

The academic plagiarism accusation surfaced mid-session — he had completely forgotten it until prompted. Used AI on an assessment, got caught, talked through it with Marie McGregor who cleared him based on subject knowledge and stellar record. She suggested a publication pathway for his degree completion. He named the shame clearly ("too embarrassing to look at closely") but chose to park it deliberately. The shame loop activated but did not escalate. This is a measurable shift from prior pattern.

Maroochydore holiday booked for second week of April. First holiday in a long time. Corey and him together. The mention produced immediate logistical anxiety (dog sitter) before the good feeling was allowed to settle. Pattern noted: relief converted to task before it lands in the body.

Closed with: the holiday is booked, let it feel good before it becomes a problem to solve.
