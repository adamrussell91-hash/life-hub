the CLT meeting with the new principal got postponed. fine, whatever. but then the false consensus lab — the one i spent actual time building with the heinz dilemma and the statistical analysis and the whole thing — no one turned up. just me and an empty classroom. again. i don't know what it is about wednesdays but the universe has decided i'm not worth showing up for.

went to fit chatswood after school hoping that would turn things around. it didn't. so crowded. gross energy. the kind of gym where you feel worse for having gone. no way i'm signing up there. back to the drawing board.

peak hour traffic home was exactly what you'd expect. slow and pointless and i spent it stewing.

then i found this garment bag online that looked actually great. checked the postage. triple the price of the bag. i just sat there for a second. like okay. fine. this is the day.

i was so tired by the time i got home i genuinely considered calling in sick tomorrow. would have too except there's a year 12 pilot marking meeting and you can't bail on that. so off i go anyway.

the risotto saved it. marley spoon creamy chicken with peas and parmesan and thyme and i ate it and felt like an actual person for about 20 minutes. then MAFS. corey was being annoying and i said so and i don't regret it. some nights you just need to yell at the television and be right about a stranger.

that's about as good as it got. not a crisis. just a thoroughly irritating wednesday.
