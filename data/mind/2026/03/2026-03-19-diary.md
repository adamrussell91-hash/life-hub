The dietitian thing though. Had to cancel the appointment with Caley because of a timetable clash and now I have to go find another one and I could scream. It's the life admin. It's the endless fucking life admin that sits on top of everything else. I genuinely fantasise about taking a day off work just to deal with all of it. Just one day where I don't have to be a teacher, I just get to be a person who handles their stuff. I'm so tired. Not sleepy tired, though that too, but the kind of tired that sits in your bones and doesn't shift even when you sleep.

The job just keeps getting harder every year. I said it out loud and I meant it. It's not any one thing, there's no single villain to point at. It's structural. You're just expected to do more now than you ever were before and nobody's taking anything off the plate to make room. Everyone around me is tired too. That's almost the worst part. It's not like I'm uniquely struggling, we're all struggling, and somehow that makes it harder to say anything because what would even change.

KFC was genuinely delightful though. The Zinger Stacker was the best decision I made all day and I'm not apologising for that.

I've always known it. The week is controlled and the weekend just kind of happens. That sucked to stare at directly.

Workout got done. Upper body, glutes, cardio, the whole thing. Whether it helped the mood I'm not sure but I did it and that counts.

I just want this weekend to be fun and actually restful. Not the kind of weekend where you do a bunch of things and arrive at Monday more exhausted than you started. I want to do things I enjoy, genuinely enjoy them, and feel like a human being by Sunday night. That's not a lot to ask. I hope the weather cooperates.
