Diary Entry: A better day because it had scenery and conversation. Ended with an oddly perfect hotel night even though dinner was a flop.
Energy: Medium
Full Diary Entry: thursday i could not deal with another shopping centre day so we kept it slower. morning coffee again. we went for a walk to a cafe we thought would be open for breakfast and it was not so we ended up eating somewhere else that turns into a bar at night. it was nice enough. it was just hot all the time. even in the morning it is warm and sticky.

angela picked us up around 10:30 and we drove up into the mountains. the views were honestly spectacular. it was really good to catch up with her properly without chris and just talk. she is very good at talking so we talked the whole way. we stopped in this little tourist town that had the weirdest german holland vibe. cuckoo clocks. thatched roofs. water wheel. it was kind of hilarious but also genuinely pretty. there was this amazing church with glass walls to take advantage of the view which was actually stunning.

i wanted to buy something but everything was tourist prices. like a leather belt for 140 dollars. no thanks. we had lunch. pizza was fine. the view did most of the work. we gave angela the wedding invite and she did the whole i cannot read it because i will cry thing and then she never read it and never said anything about it which felt weird and a bit disappointing. whatever.

we also looked at this display home you can win in a raffle. worth six million apparently. it was beautiful but felt very built for airbnb vibes. we did not buy tickets because we never win anything. then she drove us down to her place and showed us the house. chris was there working and barely said hello which i found rude as hell but ok. had more chat with angela and got all her expert opinions on everything.

she dropped us back at the hotel late afternoon and i was starving. i went and got maccas and drew a spa bath and ate double cheeseburgers in the spa bath which is honestly something i should do more often. it felt ridiculous and luxurious and perfect.

we snoozed then got ready for dinner. italian. it was basically next door to the greek place from last night and it ended up being the most disappointing dinner of the whole trip. corey’s beef cheek ragout was bland and weirdly sweet. they brought him the wrong pasta first so we waited forever. my pasta was ok but nothing special. then there was this obnoxious woman next to us talking loudly about crohn’s and saying all this wrong shit like it is basically coeliac but way worse and you cannot have gluten and whatever. i wanted to tell her to shut up but i just sat there simmering.

a garlic bread pizza that does not taste like garlic is genuinely insane. we left annoyed, got yochi again, went back to the apartment and just lounged on the couch watching tv. it was still a good day overall because the mountains were beautiful and seeing angela was nice. but yeah. the food scene here is not it.
