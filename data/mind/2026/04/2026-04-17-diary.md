Date: 17 April 2026
Diary Entry: A half holiday half admin day with a good plane ride home and a strong feeling of relief to be back.
Energy: Medium
Full Diary Entry: friday was the last day and it felt like that weird mix of still being on holidays but also already mentally shifting into getting home mode. we packed everything up and realised we were missing a key which was annoying and stressful. could not find it. just had to shrug and hope nothing goes wrong.

our flight was not until the afternoon so we left the bags behind the counter and walked to mooloolaba. it is a long walk and i was sweating like crazy because it is just hot. but mooloolaba actually has more of that small holiday coastal vibe that i expected. i liked it.

then i had that stupid realisation that i needed to do the aitsl advisory application and i had not written it. so i spent most of the morning trying to do it on my phone with claude and notion which is honestly a nightmare. i got it done in the end but it chewed up the whole morning.

on the plus side there were so many hot men running. zero complaints there. had a juice at lullabar. walked out to the wharf and around the fisheries area. it was nice.

we got rice boy for lunch because tia and angela both recommended it. it was tasty but it is ridiculous how expensive things are. one piece of bao for eighteen dollars is insane. we also got the katsu curry fries which were good.

bus back to the hotel. grabbed our bags. uber to the airport. waited around. the flight was delayed a bit. then we got on and i just loved looking out the window. watching the sunset from the air was genuinely beautiful and i took a million photos. had pringles on the plane which is now a tradition i never want to lose.

we landed around six ish. then the airport did that annoying thing where they changed the baggage carousel and everyone was standing in the wrong spot. eventually got the bags. uber home. it was late enough that the dog sitter had already left. the house smelled gross. corey went and got kfc and it was exactly what i wanted. tower burger box. crashed.

i am glad we went away and it was a good break but i do not think i loved the sunshine coast as a destination. too much staring. too many teenagers roaming around. it did not have that calm seaside vibe i wanted. but the hotel was great and it scratched the itch and it felt genuinely nice to be home again.

Rice Boy lunch was tasty despite the price
Loved watching the sunset from the plane and taking photos
Pringles on the plane tradition
KFC at home hit the spot and it felt good to be back in your own space
