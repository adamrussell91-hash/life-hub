Diary Entry: Early flight up. Cancelled taxi drama. Airport rituals. Flight. Rainy arrival. Spa day. Hotel reset. Food. Walks. Shopping. Angela day trip. Disappointing dinners. Mooloolaba walk. Flew home and felt glad to be back.

Full Diary Entry: tuesday started with barely any sleep and that horrible mix of excitement and dread that something would go wrong. of course something did. the taxi cancelled at 4:05am because the payment bounced after we switched to westpac. i fully panicked for a minute then got an uber in five minutes and it was cheaper anyway so it ended up being a win. said goodbye to the dogs and the house and got to the airport still half asleep but weirdly happy. checking in is all machines now which i loved. airport food court is still one of my favourite places because it means something fun is about to happen. maccas breakfast. then the flight. i was stuck in the middle seat and got a bit motion sick but it was fine. landed and it was raining which felt wrong for the sunshine coast. taxi to the hotel was a total rip off. dropped bags and suddenly it was warm salty air and holiday mode.

caught the bus to mooloolaba for the spa day. criminal coffee was actually great. the shops were mostly dead for me. heaps for women. nothing for men. the spa itself looked the part but the float tank was a let down. i did not realise hair colour is a problem so i had to keep my head up like an idiot with a pool noodle. the salts stung my arms so badly where the dermatitis is and my brain never shut up the whole time. it just felt boring and passive and i kept thinking what is this even doing. the massage afterwards was genuinely good though and i almost fell asleep. the facial was basically the same routine penny used to do which was funny. the acid peel stung like hell and apparently i went bright red. i kept feeling guilty about spending so much money and then walking out not feeling magically reborn. i looked like a lunatic with a hair mask and my skin was red and raw. then she tried to upsell me products. classic.

checked into the hotel and it was honestly amazing. huge. two bedrooms. full kitchen. ocean views. it felt like a proper escape base. we showered and things got a bit feral in a way that actually felt really good. low libido has been a thing and this was the first time in a while where it felt properly fun and connected and horny. then we went for mexican. the birria tacos were bland and oily but the chicken nachos were unreal. i got it on my shirt and basically ruined it which pissed me off. later we walked to toro for spanish. it was fine. trying a bit too hard and overpriced. got yochi after which was great. walked back feeling a bit on edge because there were groups of teenagers around.

wednesday was coffee again then sunshine plaza. had breakfast at a cafe that felt like it could have been the same place from schoolies years ago and i tried to recreate that old smoothie. it was fine. the plaza was huge and honestly just felt like being back in sydney. we shopped a lot and got another suitcase. the vibe was gross though. teenagers everywhere and people staring constantly. i hated that. dinner was greek and somehow bland which should be illegal. saganaki was the only good part. yochi again back at the apartment and honestly it was nice to just eat without being hyper aware of everything. i worried a bit about crohn’s but i also needed the break from constant monitoring.

thursday i could not face another shopping centre so we kept it slower. breakfast somewhere random. angela picked us up and we drove into the mountains which was actually really lovely. beautiful views and a weird little tourist town with a german vibe and cuckoo clocks and thatched roofs. lunch was fine. the view did the heavy lifting. gave angela the wedding invite and she did that thing where she said she could not read it because she would cry and then she never read it and never mentioned it again which felt weird. she showed us her place. chris barely said hi which i found rude but whatever. got back to the hotel late and i got maccas and had a spa bath with double cheeseburgers which was honestly elite. dinner was italian and it was the most disappointing meal of the trip. bland. wrong pasta brought out. loud annoying woman next to us talking absolute nonsense about crohn’s. yochi again. tv. bed.

friday we packed up and thought we lost a key. flight was late afternoon so we walked to mooloolaba which had a better coastal vibe than maroochydore. i spent half the morning trying to write that aitsl advisory application on my phone which was such a pain but i got it done. also no shortage of hot men running which was not exactly a hardship. had rice boy for lunch. expensive for what it was but tasty. bus back then uber to the airport then home. i loved looking out the window and watching the sunset from the air. had pringles on the plane which is my little tradition now. they stuffed up the baggage carousel which was annoying. got home late and the dog sitter had already gone and the house smelled rank. corey grabbed kfc and we crashed.

overall it was a nice break and i am glad we went but i do not think i loved the sunshine coast as a destination. too many people staring and too many teenagers roaming around and it did not have that chill seaside vibe i wanted. but it scratched the itch and the hotel itself was great and i do feel a bit more reset now. also it was genuinely nice to be home.
