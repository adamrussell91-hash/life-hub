Diary Entry: Early start and travel chaos that resolved well. Spa day had mixed results. Hotel was a standout. Dinner and dessert ended the day in holiday mode.
Energy: Medium
Full Diary Entry: tuesday started with barely any sleep and that gross mix of excitement and stress that something would go wrong. of course something did. the taxi cancelled at 4:05am because the payment bounced after we switched to westpac. i panicked for about a minute then booked an uber and it arrived in five minutes and was cheaper so it ended up being fine. said goodbye to the dogs and the house and we were off in the dark warm air.

the airport felt weirdly joyful. everything is automated now and i loved it. bag drop. scan. sticker. done. the domestic food court is still one of my favourite places in the world because it means you are about to do something fun. maccas breakfast. waited. then the flight. i was in the middle seat and got a bit motion sick which sucked but it was quick. we landed and it was raining which felt wrong for the sunshine coast. then the taxi to the hotel was a rip off. fifty bucks for not that far. dumped the bags and went out into the warm sea air feeling like ok we are actually here.

caught the bus to mooloolaba for my spa day. criminal coffee was genuinely good. the shops were a bit dead and mostly for women but whatever. the spa itself looked calm and fancy but the float tank was a let down. i did not realise hair colour can ruin filters so i had to keep my head up like an idiot with a pool noodle. the salts stung my arms like crazy where the dermatitis is and my brain never shut up the whole time. i kept waiting for it to switch off and it just did not. it felt boring and passive and uncomfortable.

the massage after was actually great though and i almost fell asleep. the facial was basically the same routine penny used to do which was funny because i could predict each step. the acid peel stung like hell and apparently i went super red. i walked out with a hair mask and raw skin and that stupid feeling of guilt about spending so much money without feeling magically reborn. then she tried to upsell products which just annoyed me.

checked into the hotel and it was honestly amazing. huge place. two bedrooms. full kitchen. ocean view. it immediately felt like a proper escape. we showered and things got feral in a way i have really missed lately. low libido has been a thing and this cut through it completely. it actually made me feel good in my body again for a minute.

we went for mexican. the birria tacos were bland and oily but the chicken nachos were insanely good. i got it on my shirt and basically ruined it which pissed me off. had a little snooze then got dressed and walked to toro for spanish. it was fine. trying a bit too hard and overpriced but still a nice dinner. got yochi after and walked back to the hotel feeling a bit on edge because there were groups of teenagers around. watched tv. collapsed. first day done.
