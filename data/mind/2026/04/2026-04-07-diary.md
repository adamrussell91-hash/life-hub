Stopped at Wangi Bakehouse on the way. Haven't been there in probably fifteen years, not since i actually lived there. Got a cheese and bacon pie and honestly it tasted exactly how i remembered. Corey and i split it. Weird being back. I drive through the area every couple of years just to see it but the bakehouse was different. That was a specific place from a specific time and walking back in felt like putting on a coat that doesn't quite fit anymore. Not bad. Just not comfy.

Had second breakfast with Bob after that which is very hobbit of us. Chicken avo melt. Then drove to Whitebridge and had lunch with Rachel and Logan at White Cottage. Caesar salad and a berry protein smoothie. Rachel paid which was lovely of her. It was a nice lunch. Easy company.

Then the drive home was fucking awful. I could see on the camera that the dog sitter had left the front blinds open and Leo was barking his head off at every person walking past. Which is not a disaster. It is literally just a dog being a dog. But my brain decided it was a five alarm emergency and i was anxious the entire drive back. Way more stressed than the situation required and i knew it was disproportionate even while it was happening which is its own special kind of frustrating.

Told Corey i was stressed. He was distracted and didn't really land with it. That was annoying. But i am practising not overreacting to that stuff and i think i handled it okay. Did not escalate. Did not swallow it. Just let it be annoying and moved on. That feels like progress even if it doesn't feel dramatic.

Got home. Relief. Dogs were fine obviously. Made the Marley Spoon basil pesto chicken penne for dinner and it was genuinely delicious even if Brisket will have words about the fat content.

Big thing today: bought the AEKE K1. The home gym. Actually purchased it. The number was terrifying. Like physically uncomfortable to click confirm. But grandad's inheritance came through and part of the reason it exists is so i can use it to build something. A home gym that removes the one barrier that has stopped me exercising independently for months feels like a worthy use. I hope he would think so. The responsibility of not wasting that money sits heavy though. It is not fun money. It is his life turned into a number and i do not want to be careless with it.

Also planning to go buy Muji travel minis for Maroochydore next week. Tiny beautiful toiletries for a holiday i am actually looking forward to. That feels new. Looking forward to things without immediately converting the good feeling into logistics anxiety.

The engine wants to run. I can feel the compulsion to start marking, start planning, start being productive. But i am ignoring it for another day. The marking will be there. The holiday will not.

Feeling better now. Settled. Home. Corey is hiccuping on the couch and i want to kill him but other than that, the evening is quiet and that is enough.
