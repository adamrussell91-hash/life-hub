Kate at midday. Went in expecting to talk about grief stuff and ended up spending the whole session on Corey. Told her about my little experiment where i set up a scenario to see if he would initiate sex within 24 hours. He did not. I then announced this to him like a scientist presenting findings at a conference. Kate was amused. We unpacked it a bit. Why it might be the way it is. She said something that stuck, that every couple she sees is two people trying to pull the other person closer to being more like them. Sounds so simple but i have not stopped thinking about it. We left it as an observation, not a problem to solve. That felt right.

Had about 40 minutes between Kate and Veronica so i got my oat milk cold brew with Earl Grey syrup and sat outside in North Sydney reading a book. One of those moments where i felt like i actually live in a city. Like a proper person with a proper life who does things between appointments instead of anxiously scrolling. I do actually live in a global city. That still surprises me sometimes.

EP with Veronica was massive. Squatted 30kg for the first time. Up from 20kg last session. Sled pushes replaced the split lunges and absolutely cooked my heart rate. Harder bands on the glute bridges with 19kg on my hips. Everything progressed. I think that might be the first session where i actually felt strong rather than just getting through it.

After that i did not feel like any of the Marley Spoon options so Corey and i went to BBQ Wang for Korean barbecue. Haven't been in years. We had wagyu and sweet and spicy chicken and kimchi and these little fried cheese sticks that had no business being that good. The charcoal grill ran out of gas halfway through which was hilarious because neither of us really knew what we were doing anyway. It was delicious though. Properly fun.

Then Brisket told me i had annihilated my fat goal and i felt like shit about it for about ten minutes. The Oporto burger at lunch was a third of the damage and i did not even enjoy it that much. No protein ice cream set up either because i still have not sorted the Ninja Creami. My own fault. Infrastructure gap, not a disaster, but still annoying.

Got home and watched MAFS final vows on the couch with Corey. Not much plot. Very dramatic. Absolutely loved it. The evening was just lovely. That is the whole word for it. Full of Korean food and couch and each other.

Spent bits of the day planning holidays and looking at things and thinking about Maroochydore. There is an energy at the moment that feels different. Like i am reaching for things i actually want rather than ticking off obligations. Determined to enjoy the rest of these holidays. That sentence would not have come out of my mouth six months ago.
