Date: 15 April 2026
Diary Entry: A mall heavy day with a weird vibe but some practical wins. Dinner disappointed but the overall pace still felt like a break.
Energy: Medium
Full Diary Entry: wednesday was the first full day of just being there with nothing urgent to do and i actually loved that. we went back to criminal for coffee. then we walked down to sunshine plaza because i wanted to check it out. on the way we stopped for breakfast at a cafe that felt like it could have been the same one from schoolies years ago. i tried to recreate the smoothie i used to get back then. it was fine. overpriced like everything is in a holiday town but still a nice little nostalgia moment.

sunshine plaza is massive. myer. david jones. everything. it honestly just felt like being back in sydney which made me a bit like why are we here. we shopped a lot though. myer had a decent sale and i got some clothes which felt like a win. realised we needed another suitcase so we bought one at target. practical holiday admin but still satisfying.

the vibe in the shopping centre sucked though. gangs of teenagers everywhere and they are just disgusting. and people stare. not subtle. just full on staring at you like you are a spectacle. it makes my skin crawl. we stopped for a coffee when we got tired and i looked at the high ropes course which actually looked really cool. had a hot dog at this place that was trying really hard to be a new york deli even though it is literally in a shopping centre. it was tasty though.

we ubered back to the hotel and chilled for a while. showered. got dressed. walked to dinner through that new cbd area and i could not get over how much maroochydore has changed. it has exploded. it does not feel like a quiet seaside town at all. we stopped at buderim distillery on the way and corey had a gin flight. i had juice. had some chippies which were nice.

dinner was greek and it was such a disappointment. bland as fuck. greek food should not be bland. where is the lemon. where is the oregano. it felt criminal. the saganaki was the one good thing and even that came with jam on top which is just weird but ok it tasted good. everything was overpriced again.

we got yochi again and took it back to the apartment to watch tv. honestly i have been eating whatever i want whenever i want and it feels like a break from the constant food monitoring. i do worry about crohn’s a bit but i also think some of the burnout is from being hyper aware of everything i eat. so letting it go for a few days feels good. went to bed feeling a bit flat about the destination but still glad we are away.
Highlights: Slow morning coffee and no real time pressure which felt luxurious
Shopping win at Myer sale and sorted a new suitcase
The high ropes course looked cool
Saganaki was good despite the rest being bland
Yochi again and relaxing back at the apartment
Freedom to eat without micromanaging everything felt like burnout relief
