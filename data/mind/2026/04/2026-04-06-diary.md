Stopped at Charlestown Square for lunch. Got a Schnitz wrap which hit the spot. Picked up a candle for Rachel as a housewarming present for her new place at Fishing Point.

Then we went to Belmont to see Donna and Jo and Dave. Cheese and biscuits, just sitting around. Quiet and easy. No drama, no performance, just family doing what family does. It was nice. Sometimes that is all you need honestly, just people who know you sitting around eating cheese.

Drove out to Fishing Point after that and Rachel's new place is lovely. She and Brinn have done well for themselves. Amy came over too which was a throwback, haven't seen her in ages. We just hung out and it was chill. Really chill.

Being back at the lake was weird though. Like properly weird. All these places I knew from growing up but seeing them through completely different eyes. I am not the same person who lived here and the landscape knows it even if it looks the same. Not a bad feeling. Just a strange one.

Corey seemed to enjoy himself which was good. He was relaxed. I was relaxed. Woke up to lake views this morning and it was one of those moments where everything just felt simple and right. Good day.
