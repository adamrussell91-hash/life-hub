## Historical Entry — Ported from Day One

*Period: mid-2020. Adam was 29–30. COVID lockdown in Australia.*

---

During the COVID lockdown period, Adam's journal entries reflect the particular exhaustion and stasis of that time. He wrote of a loop he couldn't quite break: tired, cooking elaborate things, eating them, feeling heavy, repeat.

Cooking emerged as a significant coping mechanism — something creative and productive he could do within confined circumstances. There is warmth in those entries about food and the kitchen. But the cost was a sense of physical heaviness and the familiar shame about body and weight cycling back.

**Key themes from this period:**

- Cooking as creative expression and control in an uncontrollable environment
- A "tired and fat" loop — his own framing — where fatigue led to comfort eating which led to body shame which led to more fatigue
- The enforced togetherness of lockdown with Corey (mostly positive, but also intense)
- Uncertainty about career, future, what comes next

**Psychological significance:** The 2020 lockdown didn't produce acute crisis for Adam — he had Corey, he had the flat, he was fundamentally safe. But the patterns here — using food as comfort, the weight-fatigue shame cycle, creative work as coping — are established features of his emotional toolkit that persist into 2025 and beyond.

For Penelope: this period is worth noting as evidence that Adam functions reasonably well in enforced stillness when his primary relationship is secure. The 2025 crisis wasn't about stillness — it was about the combination of dislocation AND relational separation.
