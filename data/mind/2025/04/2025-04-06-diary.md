## Historical Entry — Ported from Day One

*Original date: 6 April 2025. Adam was 34, spending a day alone at Manly Beach, Sydney.*

---

Adam spent the day alone at Manly. It was clearly a good day in many objective respects — a beautiful beach suburb, sunshine, the particular quality of an Australian autumn afternoon by the water.

And yet he wrote: **"I just wish I had Corey."**

This entry captures a specific and poignant emotional experience: the way good things, when experienced alone, can deepen rather than relieve longing. The beauty of Manly wasn't comfort — it was a reminder of what was absent. The better the day, the sharper the wish to share it.

**This entry also has a particular significance:** today (6 April 2026) is exactly one year later. Adam has now lived a full year since this entry. Whatever has changed or hasn't changed in that year, this was the starting point of spring 2025.

**Psychological significance:** Adam doesn't experience loneliness as grey and featureless. He experiences it in contrast — against brightness, beauty, good days. The absence is sharpest when everything else is good. This is an important clinical note: Adam may minimise his loneliness on difficult days ("at least I have this") while feeling it most acutely on good ones.

For Penelope: this entry type — 'good day, but alone' — may be worth watching for in future journal entries. It's a specific emotional signature.
