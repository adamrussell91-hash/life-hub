## Historical Entry — Ported from Day One

*Original date: 24 October 2025. Adam was 35. Mystery illness onset.*

---

Adam's blood tests came back with alarming results: white blood cell count elevated to approximately three times the normal range, with impaired liver function markers also present. Doctors did not have an immediate explanation.

He wrote of experiencing **"moments of terror."**

This phrase is precise. Not sustained terror — moments of it. Suggesting that he was managing, functioning, getting through the day, but that fear was breaking through in waves. The uncertainty was particularly difficult: a clear diagnosis, even a serious one, would have been easier than not knowing.

**Context of this period:**

- He was still in Sydney, still geographically separated from his primary home with Corey
- He was already emotionally depleted from the 2025 crisis that had been building since March
- Now physical health was added to the burden
- He was facing the possibility of serious illness without his primary support person physically present

**Psychological significance:** This entry marks the beginning of the most acutely difficult period documented in the journal record. Adam was dealing with: professional misery, relational separation, loneliness, AND now physical fear simultaneously. The load was extraordinary.

For Vera: the 'moments of terror' framing is Adam at his most controlled under pressure. He is not catastrophising here — he is accurately naming a genuinely frightening medical situation. Distinguish between this and anxiety spirals: this was real fear about real unknowns.
