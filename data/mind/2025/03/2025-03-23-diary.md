## Historical Entry — Ported from Day One

*Original dates: 22–23 March 2025. Adam was 34, in Sydney, separated from Corey.*

---

Two entries from around the same time, written at or near Zarraffa's Coffee, contain the emotional core of the 2025 crisis in two sentences:

**"I miss my life. I miss the emotional comfort."**

**"I'm lonely."**

These entries are remarkable in their plainness. Adam, who typically writes with elaboration and self-analysis, stripped everything back here to the simplest possible statement of what was true. No framing, no theorising, no attempting to make it make sense. Just: this is what it is.

He was sitting in a coffee shop in a city that wasn't his, missing the life he'd built with Corey — the domestic warmth, the emotional ease, the sense of being held by a shared world. The loneliness wasn't about being without people in general. It was about being without the specific person and the specific life that made him feel like himself.

**Psychological significance:** These may be the most emotionally unguarded entries in the entire journal record. The fact that he dropped the analytical voice entirely and just wrote 'I'm lonely' suggests the feeling had broken through whatever containment he usually maintained. This is the raw centre of the 2025 experience.

For Vera: Adam's loneliness is relational and specific — it's about Corey and the home they built together. It is not a generalised social anxiety or isolation. When he is separated from that specific bond, he experiences something close to grief. This entry is the evidence.
