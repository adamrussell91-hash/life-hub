## Historical Entry — Ported from Day One

*Original date: 9 November 2025. Adam returning to his Sydney apartment after a visit with Corey.*

---

Adam had been with Corey — presumably in their home together, or during a visit — and had returned to the Sydney apartment.

He wrote: **"I don't know if I could do two years of this."**

This is a sentence of enormous significance. It suggests:

1. That the arrangement (Sydney posting, separation from Corey) was understood at the time to potentially continue for up to two years
2. That the visit with Corey had made everything both better (the time together was grounding) and worse (returning to the apartment after felt harder in contrast)
3. That Adam was questioning his ability to continue enduring the situation

**The 'coming back to the apartment' context:**

The apartment, post-visit, would have been particularly difficult. The contrast between 'with Corey' and 'back in Sydney alone' made the separation more acute, not less. This is a known psychological pattern: visits can temporarily relieve longing while making the return harder.

**Psychological significance:** This is one of the clearest expressions of the cumulative toll of the 2025 arrangement. Adam was not sure he could sustain it. He was still trying, still enduring, but the capacity was visibly eroding.

For Vera: this entry is important for understanding how close to breaking point Adam was in November 2025. The fact that he framed it as a genuine uncertainty ('I don't know if I could') rather than a dramatic statement ('I cannot do this') suggests characteristic understatement.
