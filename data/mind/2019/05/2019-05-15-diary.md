## Historical Entry — Ported from Day One

*Original date: 15 May 2019. Adam was approximately 28–29, placed in a country teaching post far from home.*

---

Adam wrote: **"So homesick, work is really shit, wish I could live at home."**

This entry is significant because it shows that the dislocation and longing for home that would characterise the 2025 Sydney crisis predates it by at least six years. The same emotional signature — geographical separation from Corey and the familiar, difficult work environment, longing to be somewhere else — appears here in an earlier form.

The country posting was a professional obligation or choice that placed him physically far from his support structure. He found the work itself difficult and unrewarding in this context. The homesickness was visceral, not just abstract — he wanted the physical comfort of home.

**Psychological significance:** This is not Adam's first experience of misalignment between where his body was and where his heart wanted to be. The pattern of enduring difficult circumstances away from home for professional reasons while privately suffering is a recurring one. By 2025, it would become much more intense and the stakes much higher — but the template was established here.

For Vera: Adam has a strong sense of place and home as emotional anchor. Separation from that anchor reliably produces significant distress. This is not fragility; it's a consistent and knowable feature of his psychology.
